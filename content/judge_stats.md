## Business email compromise and payment redirection

The RCMP describes **Scheme #2** as fraudsters impersonating suppliers to redirect invoice payments to new bank accounts. Their business email compromise page states that BEC is **one of the most financially damaging online crimes**.  
Source: [RCMP — Business email compromise](https://www.rcmp.ca/en/federal-policing/cybercrime/cyber-features/business-email-compromise)

The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre reported that in **2024**, **spear-phishing** accounted for about **CAD $67.5 million** in reported losses, while **total fraud** reported to CAFC was about **CAD $647 million**, with only **5–10%** of fraud estimated to be reported.  
Source: [CAFC news release, 2025-07-24](https://antifraudcentre.ca/news-nouvelles/2025/2025-07-24-eng.htm)

The CAFC **2024 annual statistics report** (PDF) lists **processed loss** of **$643,716,736** (figure as published in that report).  
Source: [CAFC annual stats report 2024 (EN PDF)](https://open.canada.ca/data/dataset/69c68f22-8a2a-43d1-8f4e-4017e3ffebba/resource/1ffc4a5e-e41b-48c7-929e-633d023ef8dc/download/cafc-annual-stats-report-2024-en.pdf)

CISA advisory **AA22-340A** describes a **food and agriculture sector** payment-redirection pattern (business email compromise targeting suppliers). It is a **pattern advisory**, not a Canada-specific agriculture loss total.  
Source: [CISA AA22-340A](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa22-340a)

## Cargo theft and cold chain (Canada / North America)

CargoNet reported **2025** estimated cargo theft losses in the **United States and Canada** of nearly **USD $725 million**, with **food and beverage** theft incidents up about **47%** year over year (as stated in their 2025 theft trends material).  
Source: [CargoNet — 2025 theft trends](https://www.cargonet.com/news-and-events/cargonet-in-the-media/2025-theft-trends/)

Trailer thefts **nearly doubled** in **2025** according to industry reporting summarized by TruckNews. **Équité** recorded about **1,601** thefts in **Ontario** versus about **240** in **Alberta** and about **180** in **Quebec** (tractors, trailers, and cargo combined in that reporting). Ontario is the **large majority** of those three provinces’ recorded thefts in that comparison—do not quote an “85%” figure; it is not in these sources. Cargo losses remain **underreported**.  
Source: [TruckNews — trailer theft and underreporting, 2025](https://www.trucknews.com/security/canadas-trailer-theft-nearly-doubles-as-cargo-losses-go-underreported-in-2025/1003209612/)

## Double brokering

The Canadian International Freight Forwarders Association (CIFFA) briefing states that **double brokering** in the Canadian trucking industry is **“now rampant.”**  
Source: [CIFFA — Double brokering in the Canadian trucking industry (PDF)](https://www.ciffa.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/230403-Double-Brokering-in-the-Canadian-Trucking-Industry_Final.pdf)
