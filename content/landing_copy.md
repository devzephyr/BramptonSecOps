# SupplyChek landing copy

## Sealed thread first

Most fraud in Canadian cold chains starts in the inbox: an email, a PDF rate con, or a screenshot from a driver group. SupplyChek answers with a sealed in-app thread. Write the change, pick the request type, and see plain-language flags and a playbook—before anyone changes banking, a dock, or a portal setting.

## Policy second

Your rules live next to the composer: who must approve bank changes, which domains are on file, and when two people need to sign off. SupplyChek does not replace your policy; it makes the right call the easy one when someone is rushing a Mississauga pickup or a Peel-region diversion.

## Portal later

When you are ready, partners can submit through a passkey-protected portal. Until then, the desk workflow works with sealed-note review. No shared-secret sign-in. No chat-app side channel for approvals.

## Built for Canadian SMEs

Designed for cold stores, carriers, and distributors moving food and beverage in Ontario: Lake Ontario shoreline suppliers, Mississauga DCs, Brampton cross-docks, and Peel-region yards. Language is grade 8 on purpose so floor, dispatch, and finance share the same checklist.

## Sign-in model

Passkeys only for SupplyChek accounts. Your partners keep using their existing processes; SupplyChek helps your staff verify strange requests without teaching everyone a new chat tool.

## What we do not claim

Mail-authentication passes and encrypted transport do not prove a request is real. SupplyChek shows heuristics and playbooks so your team calls the number already on file—every time payment details, destinations, or access are on the line.
